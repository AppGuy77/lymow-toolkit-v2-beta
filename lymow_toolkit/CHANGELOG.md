Lymow Toolkit v2.1.6-beta

**Beta on its own download channel** — recommended for now to people running two or more mowers. It installs
over your current Toolkit (sign-in, maps, settings and history are kept). To go back, install the latest stable release from the
stable releases page: https://github.com/AppGuy77/lymow-toolkit-downloads/releases/latest


- Fixed: the satellite-imagery alignment looked reset from any other browser or phone. It is now one setting per location (or for the whole account), shared by every mower in that yard, every browser, the camera mini-maps and the phone link; your existing alignment is carried over on the first start.
- Fixed: the away link, a phone or a second PC opened with the mower list but stayed "offline" and never connected. Every browser now opens on the mower the Toolkit shows and connects at once, Fleet Mode included.
- Fixed: settings that lived only in the browser (Satellite / Street map / provider, the map overlays and zone freshness, calendar view, camera switches, remote drive speed, joystick size, remembered cut height, Settings section, saved mow order, multi-pass set-up) are now saved on the Toolkit itself, so every browser shows the same.
- Fixed: "reconnecting…" now shows the reason and code when the live link is refused, and the Toolkit logs it.


This beta channel updates independently of the stable Toolkit; report anything odd on the beta repo's Issues page.
