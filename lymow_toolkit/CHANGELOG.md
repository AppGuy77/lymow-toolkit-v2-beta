Lymow Toolkit v2.0.4-beta

**Beta on its own download channel** — recommended for now to people running two or more mowers. It installs
over your current Toolkit (sign-in, maps, settings and history are kept). To go back, install v1.54.2 from the
stable releases page: https://github.com/AppGuy77/lymow-toolkit-downloads/releases/latest


- Fixed: the camera grid works on the FIRST open — each camera retries its direct WiFi link before falling back, so the "Windows can't show the WiFi picture" message no longer appears on the first open.
- Fixed: turning on Fleet Mode shows every mower at the location on the first toggle (no refresh needed).
- Fixed: two docking stations close together both show on the map — docks merge only within 2 feet.


This beta channel updates independently of the stable Toolkit; report anything odd on the beta repo's Issues page.
